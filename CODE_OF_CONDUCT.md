# Code of Conduct

CoPets contributors must keep project spaces safe, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when disagreeing about code, design, or project direction.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith until evidence says otherwise.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's prompts, logs, identifiers, credentials, or private
  communications.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation without personal attacks.
- Help make contribution paths usable for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private
data, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces or project-related
private communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Use the repository's private maintainer-reporting or moderation channel for conduct reports. Do not
publish sensitive reports in an issue. If the repository does not yet expose a private channel,
contact a maintainer privately through an existing trusted channel.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue and review systems, release spaces, and when someone is
officially representing CoPets.
